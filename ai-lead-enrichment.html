FAQ

Lead Qualification and CRM Enrichment FAQ

Learn how the service handles incoming or existing leads, applies qualification logic and updates connected CRM systems.

Does this service find new prospects for me?

No. This service works with incoming or already-collected leads from forms, spreadsheets, databases, campaigns or connected business systems. Outbound prospect sourcing is not included.

Does every plan use artificial intelligence?

No. Core uses transparent rule-based validation and qualification. Growth and Elite can add selective AI-assisted scoring, fit levels, confidence indicators and explanations according to the approved scope.

How are leads qualified?

Qualification is based on criteria approved by your business, such as required information, budget, company size, industry, location, role, service fit or other relevant conditions.

Can the workflow connect to my CRM?

Many implementations can connect to CRM platforms, spreadsheets, databases, forms and APIs. Compatibility, access requirements and any third-party costs are reviewed before implementation.

What happens to incomplete or uncertain leads?

They can be assigned an incomplete or review status, routed to a manual-review queue, excluded from automatic CRM creation or handled through another agreed exception path.

What information can be added to CRM records?

Depending on the selected plan and available data sources, the workflow can add validation results, qualification status, fit level, score, confidence, explanation, company information, routing details and review status.

Can the qualification logic be customized?

Yes. The rules, scoring criteria, CRM fields, routing paths, review conditions and reporting requirements are configured according to the approved implementation scope.

Can the workflow be expanded later?

Yes. Additional lead sources, CRM fields, scoring logic, review paths, integrations or connected workflows can be added after a new scope review and testing plan.
