Production Implementation Options

Make · n8n · Zapier · APIs

The reminder architecture can be adapted to Make, n8n, Zapier or custom APIs according to the customer’s data source, WhatsApp provider, CRM, message volume, security requirements and approved scope.

  • Approved WhatsApp Business provider and templates
  • Customer-specific reminder timing and eligibility rules
  • CRM, billing or database connections
  • Optional fallback and escalation workflows
  • Monitoring, message volume and maintenance

Make is the validated orchestration platform for the published SIM-03 routes, including staged WhatsApp reminders and expired-renewal escalation to HubSpot. n8n, Zapier, custom APIs and SMS fallback remain scope-dependent production options unless separately tested and documented.
