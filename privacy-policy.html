How We Collect, Use and Protect Information

This Privacy Policy explains how AutomateSuite collects, uses and protects information submitted through our website and services.

1. Introduction

AutomateSuite respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information voluntarily provided through our contact forms, setup requests and custom project requests.

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Company Information
  • Project Requirements

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, provide requested services, evaluate project requirements and improve our services.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

AutomateSuite may use cookies and local storage technologies to remember language preferences, theme preferences and improve user experience.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information submitted through our website.

Access to submitted information is limited to authorized personnel. While no online system can guarantee absolute security, we apply appropriate safeguards to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, disclosure or misuse.

  • SSL/TLS encrypted connections
  • Restricted access to submitted data
  • Secure third-party service providers
  • Regular review of security practices

6. Third-Party Services

AutomateSuite may use third-party to transmit website form submissions and may use hosting, email and content-delivery providers to operate the website and respond to requests.

7. Your Rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your information where applicable.
