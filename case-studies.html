Our Validation Approach

From Operational Challenge to Tested Workflow

Each simulation was built and tested through a structured process designed to verify the workflow logic, connected actions and exception handling.

01

Define the Scenario

Identify the operational problem, expected workflow behaviour and decision rules.

02

Connect the Tools

Configure the required applications, fields, triggers, routes and processing actions.

03

Test Multiple Paths

Run valid, incomplete, urgent and failure cases to verify both successful actions and exception handling.

04

Document the Results

Capture the workflow configuration, resulting records and connected-system confirmations.
