WhatsApp Renewal Reminders with SMS Fallback and HubSpot Escalation

See how AutomateSuite validated a workflow that evaluates renewal dates, sends staged WhatsApp reminders, includes configured SMS fallback branches and records HubSpot escalation results.

Workflow type Renewal monitoring and customer reminders
Validated stack Airtable · Make · Twilio · WhatsApp · HubSpot
Validation scope Staged reminders, configured fallback branches and escalation

Validated Simulation, Not a Customer Performance Claim

This case study documents a controlled AutomateSuite test. It demonstrates validated reminder and escalation behaviour and documents configured fallback branches, but it is not presented as a customer deployment, guaranteed delivery rate or production-scale result.

Business Challenge

Renewal Dates Can Be Missed Without Structured Follow-Up

When expiration dates and reminders are managed manually, customers may receive messages too late, delivery failures may remain unnoticed and unresolved cases may lack a clear escalation record.

Before Automation

Manual Renewal Monitoring

  • Expiration dates checked individually
  • Reminder timing depends on manual follow-up
  • Failed messages may require separate investigation
  • Escalation status updated manually
After Automation

Staged Reminder and Escalation Logic

  • Renewal dates evaluated automatically
  • Seven-day, three-day, one-day and expiration-day routes separated
  • Fallback delivery available when required
  • Processing and escalation outcomes recorded
Automation Objective

Send the Right Reminder at the Right Renewal Stage

The simulation was designed to identify eligible renewal records, mark them as processing, evaluate the remaining number of days, send the appropriate WhatsApp reminder and preserve delivery or escalation results in Airtable.

Workflow Overview

How the Renewal Reminder Workflow Operates

The scenario evaluates active renewal records, routes them according to the expiration stage and updates Airtable after each reminder, fallback or escalation action.

Renewal Evaluation

Airtable Calculates the Renewal Stage

The record contains the customer, expiration date, calculated remaining days and renewal status required by the routing logic.

Staged WhatsApp Reminders

Customers Receive Reminders at Different Renewal Stages

The tested workflow uses separate routes for seven-day, three-day, one-day and expiration-day reminders, allowing the message to reflect the remaining time.

03
Three-Day Processing Result

Airtable Records the Successful Three-Day Reminder

After the three-day WhatsApp route completes, the workflow stores the selected channel, delivery status, reminder stage and processing result.

04
One-Day Processing Result

Airtable Records the One-Day Reminder Result

After the one-day route completes, the renewal record stores the reminder stage, channel result, processing message and latest action time.

05
Expiration-Day Reminder

The Expiration-Day Route Sends the Final Reminder

When the calculated remaining time reaches zero, the workflow selects the expiration-day message and records the final reminder stage.

06
Incident Escalation

The Expired Renewal Is Escalated to HubSpot

When the configured incident conditions remain unresolved, the workflow creates a HubSpot ticket so the renewal case can be investigated and followed.

Connected-System Confirmation

The Expired Renewal Becomes a HubSpot Ticket

HubSpot confirms that the expired renewal was converted into a trackable follow-up ticket containing the customer, service and expiration context.

Validated Test Results

What the Simulation Demonstrated

The following behaviours were observed during the controlled AutomateSuite test.

Renewal Dates Evaluated

The workflow selected records according to their calculated expiration stage.

Staged Reminders Sent

The tested seven-day, three-day, one-day and expiration-day routes used their configured WhatsApp reminders.

Fallback Route Configured

The workflow architecture includes SMS fallback branches for configured delivery conditions.

Incident Escalation Created

The configured unresolved case produced a trackable HubSpot ticket.

What This Case Study Does Not Claim

The simulation does not claim guaranteed message delivery, customer renewal conversion, production-scale volume, customer return on investment or identical results for every implementation.

Operational Benefits

A More Structured Renewal Communication Process

Renewal stages are evaluated according to configured date conditions.

Separate Delivery Routes

Fallback Prepared

Validated Technology Stack

Tools Used in This Simulation

The demonstrated workflow was built and tested with the following connected platforms.

Airtable

Stores customer renewal dates, reminder stages, channel status and processing results.

Make

Orchestrates date evaluation, routing, messaging, fallback and escalation.

Twilio

Provides the tested WhatsApp Sandbox connection and the SMS connection used by the configured fallback architecture.

WhatsApp

Receives the staged renewal reminders during the controlled test.

HubSpot

Receives unresolved reminder incidents as trackable tickets.

The production stack can be adapted according to the client’s approved messaging providers, customer systems and technical requirements.
