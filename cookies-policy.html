1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function correctly, remember user preferences and improve the browsing experience.

AutomateSuite uses cookies to improve website performance, remember language and theme preferences, analyze visitor activity and enhance the overall user experience.
