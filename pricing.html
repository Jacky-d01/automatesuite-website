Pricing FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which plan should I choose?

Starter is ideal for small businesses beginning their automation journey, Growth is recommended for growing companies needing scalable workflows, and Elite is designed for advanced automation and AI-powered operations.

Should I choose a bundle or an individual service?

If you need more than one service, the bundle plans are the most cost-effective option and ensure everything works together smoothly. If you only have one immediate need, you can start with an individual service and upgrade later.

Can I combine multiple services?

Yes, you can subscribe to individual services separately. However, bundles are designed to save costs and simplify integration, so they're recommended if you plan to use multiple services.

What happens after I submit a request?

Once we receive your request, we'll review your requirements and contact you to confirm the details. After approval, we begin planning and implementation based on your selected service or bundle.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time as your needs evolve. Most clients start small and move to Growth or bundle plans once they see results.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No, everything is designed to be simple to use, and we guide you through setup. For more complex needs, we can also handle implementation for you.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. Optional maintenance and support plans are available to help monitor, update and improve your automations as your business grows.

How long does setup take?

Core setups typically take around 4 days. Growth setups range between 5–10 days. Elite and bundle setups can take up to 15–20 days depending on complexity. We focus on delivering fully tested and reliable systems rather than rushed implementations.

Are there hidden fees?

No, pricing is transparent. What you see in each tier is what you get. If you need something extra, we'll clearly define it in advance.

What if none of the plans fit my needs?

Our Enterprise & Custom Solutions option is designed for unique business requirements. We can combine services, integrate existing tools and build tailored workflows based on your specific goals.

Can the automations integrate with my existing tools?

Yes. Many automation solutions can integrate with CRMs, APIs, email platforms, spreadsheets and other business tools depending on your requirements.
